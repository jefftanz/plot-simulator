Paint Plotter Sim

Project

Saves the canvas, paint (still editable), brush paths, color guide, palette and settings in one file.

Tool

Selected paint

Drag to move, drag an end handle to reshape a straight line. Arrow keys nudge (Shift = 10×).

Path

Or drop an SVG file onto the canvas.

Color guide

A reference layer showing where colors change. It's never painted. Press G to show or hide it.

Paint

Click a swatch to paint with it, double-click to change it.

Surface tension high: edges stay where the paint was laid and ridges round up; low: paint wets the canvas and puddles. These settings apply to new paint; change placed paint in Selected paint.

Paint lines from an SVG

Each line in the file becomes a paint line. Thickness and viscosity come from the settings above.
